How I structure my Rails apps, and why.

I've been writing Rails apps for over a decade, and have established some patterns for apps that have served teams I have worked with and myself fairly well.

This repo is intended as a place for me to document these patterns, as well as provide resources for steering either Junior Developers or AI Agents in the right direction.

The Rails community is opinionated for good reason, and this guide follows suit. These opinions might change as new ideas and technologies come into play, so nothing is gospel here.

Hope this at least helps remove some friction between teams.

## What's here

- **[The guide](guide/)**: how my Rails apps are laid out and the conventions I follow, from models to CI.
- **[Patterns](patterns/)**: ways to solve problems that come up when you follow the guide, e.g. getting a value back from an action.
- **[Agent instructions](agent-instructions/)**: instruction files you can copy into your own repo, so GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, Codex and other coding agents follow the same conventions.
